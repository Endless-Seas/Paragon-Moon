<b>The year is 1212.</b> The world is caught in endless turmoil. You, of all people, happen to find yourself in the Barony of Northwich. An archipelago between two disparate continents.
<br>
<br>
Centuries ago, the Barony of Northwich had been a place of constant death and decay. An archipelago of fire and contempt. A hellscape in which the ancient Empire and Confederacy both sent thousands of mortals. To bleed, weep and be slain over the battlefields that littered the isles. Conflict for the sake of conflict. Yet, of that multi-decade long strife, a new power arose. The ever powerful Three. Watchful and courageous. The forever Baron and their two companions. 
<br>
It is said that both major powers, in an immediate cessation of violence, fled the isle. Returning to their homelands.
<br>
<br>
That is how history will speak of the isle. It is, almost assuredly, all you know if it. The Barony of Northwich, as it is now known, has seen prosperity and relative calm for those centuries that the Baron has been in power. A continuous rule, unbroken. Some speak of divinity. The locals claim this has always been 'their Baron'. None know the truth, beyond The Three, who all share the same gift. The Three who return, no matter death, tumble or loss. A power so absolute that none have attempted to challenge it and succeed. Many have tried, certainly, yet more have failed.
<br>
<br>
We are well beyond the Age of Enlightenment, now. Stagnation having long since taken hold after the archipelago became a stopper to expansion. However, both the Confederacy and Empire once more beat the war drums, in an attempt to encourage unrest. An effort to begin conflicts anew.
<br>
<br>
Two sides to a conflict that will engulf the world, in the coming years. With the archipelago, and The Three, at the middle of it all. Our home of Northwich set to be the stage for the war.
<br>
<br>
<br>
<b>The gods will be watching. They will find us wanting.</b>
